### Increment 3 — Controlled placement release (ACCEPTED DESIGN; IMPLEMENTATION NEXT)

Provide an independently invoked operator action that works while the initial SDL launcher continues supervising its child. It selects an existing broker-owned managed ID, not a raw caller-chosen UUID or currently active window. The operator may choose to place a viewer displaying remote GDM; the policy must not equate remote GUI usability with accurate local window placement.

Headless positive/negative tests precede separately approved maintainer-native placement tests; no configured `-o` RDP workflow in this increment.
